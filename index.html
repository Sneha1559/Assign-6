More than a fruit

One fruit.
Many possibilities.

Grapes belong to the genus Vitis. Different varieties are cultivated for different purposes, depending on their flavor, texture, growing conditions and intended use.

Some are best enjoyed fresh, some are grown for winemaking, and others are dried to create raisins.

01   Table Grapes

Fresh fruit varieties selected for eating, texture, sweetness and convenience.

02   Wine Grapes

Varieties cultivated for their juice composition, aroma, acidity and suitability for fermentation.

03   Raisin Grapes

Grapes dried after harvesting to create a naturally sweet ingredient for snacks and recipes.

Every grape has a story.

From vineyards and orchards to tables around the world, grape cultivation connects agriculture, food, culture and tradition. Explore a variety and discover what makes it unique.
